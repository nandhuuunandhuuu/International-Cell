About Us

Objective

To achieve excellence in multi-disciplinary areas, the International Cell (I Cell) aspires to establish significant academic and research links and collaborations with leading higher education institutes, universities and corporations across the world.

What does the Cell do?

The goal of these international partnerships is to give our students the most enriching global academic exposure possible, closing the global knowledge gap. I Cell creates opportunities by organizing student exchange programs, internships, summer schools at international universities and more, so students can live and learn in cross-cultural settings and grow in academic rigor and cultural wisdom. These programs let students study at premier institutions in other countries, and the network they build will benefit them throughout their lives.

  • Strategic alliances provide opportunities for engagement with overseas institutes and leading enterprises.
  • Academic coordination and research cooperation are established through a memorandum of understanding (MoU).
  • Final-year students get help to enter world-class universities, from subject selection to visa application.
  • Second, third and fourth-year students can join two-week or semester-long programs abroad more easily.
  • Training programs in English (IELTS / TOEFL / PTE), GRE and other courses remove the barriers to overseas higher education.

Process flow

  1. I Cell notifies students about each international opportunity.
  2. Students interested in overseas higher education register with I Cell.
  3. Registered students are trained to succeed in the English and GRE exams.
  4. I Cell helps them write their statement of purpose, which is critical for admission.
  5. I Cell helps them choose their preferred degree and the best university based on QS rankings.
  6. Each stage of the application is explained, and the status is communicated to them.
  7. Once they receive an unconditional offer, I Cell helps with visa processing.
  8. I Cell helps students complete a two-month internship or a one-semester program abroad.
